# picoCTF

## Overview

picoCTF is a beginner-friendly Capture The Flag learning platform with challenges across web, cryptography, forensics, reverse engineering, binary, and general skills. It is often recommended for students because many challenges are approachable and designed for learning.

## Why it matters in CTFs

[[picoCTF]] helps beginners practice core [[CTF]] habits: reading prompts carefully, taking notes, testing ideas legally, and learning from hints or writeups after a sincere attempt. It is a good starting point before moving into harder platforms.
