# Hack The Box Academy

## Overview

Hack The Box Academy is a structured cybersecurity education platform with modules, exercises, and practical labs. It is more course-like than a traditional CTF scoreboard.

## Why it matters in CTFs

[[Hack The Box Academy]] helps learners study foundations before tackling harder [[CTF]] tasks. Its modules can support topics such as [[Web Security]], [[Cryptography]], [[Red Team Basics]], and [[Blue Team Basics]].
