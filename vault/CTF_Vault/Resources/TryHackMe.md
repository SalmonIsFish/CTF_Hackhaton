# TryHackMe

## Overview

TryHackMe is a guided cybersecurity learning platform with rooms and learning paths for beginners through intermediate learners. It combines explanations, exercises, and lab environments.

## Why it matters in CTFs

[[TryHackMe]] helps learners build the background knowledge that makes [[CTF]] challenges less mysterious. It is especially useful for structured introductions to [[Networking Basics]], [[Web Security]], [[Linux Commands]], and defensive concepts.
