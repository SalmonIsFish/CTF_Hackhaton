# OverTheWire

## Overview

OverTheWire is a collection of wargames that teach security fundamentals, especially Linux, command-line skills, files, permissions, and careful step-by-step problem solving.

## Why it matters in CTFs

[[OverTheWire]] helps beginners build practical comfort with [[Linux Commands]], reading files, navigating directories, and thinking methodically. Those habits transfer directly into [[CTF]] challenges across many categories.
