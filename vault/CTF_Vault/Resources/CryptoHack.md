# CryptoHack

## Overview

CryptoHack is a cryptography learning platform with challenges that start from encodings and grow into more advanced mathematical cryptography topics.

## Why it matters in CTFs

[[CryptoHack]] is useful for [[Crypto]] CTF preparation because it builds recognition and reasoning around [[Encoding]], [[Encryption]], [[Hashing]], [[AES]], and [[RSA]] in a legal practice setting.
