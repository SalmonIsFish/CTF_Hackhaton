# CTF

## Overview

CTF means Capture the Flag, a legal cybersecurity learning game built around solving puzzles and submitting flags. For beginners, the simplest way to approach [[CTF]] is to ask what role it plays: does it describe data, a tool, a file, a network behavior, a security idea, or a learning category? Once you know that role, the concept becomes much less mysterious.

A good study habit is to write down what you can directly observe before making assumptions. Many cybersecurity words sound advanced, but they usually point to practical questions: what is this, where did it come from, what does it affect, and what other evidence is connected to it?

## Why it matters in CTFs

In [[CTF]] learning, [[CTF]] matters because it gives beginners a shared word for a common idea. CTFs reward careful reading, curiosity, and safe practice inside legal challenge environments. Understanding the concept helps connect clues across web, crypto, forensics, networking, and red-team style notes without turning the learning into real-world misuse.
